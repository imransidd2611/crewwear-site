FAQ

Questions, answered

What is CrewWear Custom?

CrewWear Custom makes custom full-print work uniforms for US trade businesses. We print your logo, colours and phone number edge to edge on polyester hoodies, long sleeves, polos, tees and caps for HVAC, plumbing, roofing, landscaping and pressure washing crews. The design is free and arrives in 24 hours with no setup fees. Delivery to your door usually takes 15 to 20 days after you approve your design and pay the deposit, depending on your order. Orders of $200 or more ship free.

What is sublimation printing?

Dye turns to gas and bonds into polyester fibres, so the print becomes part of the fabric. It works on polyester only.

Is the design really free?

Yes. You get a full mockup in 24 hours with no obligation to order.

What is the minimum order?

Tell us your crew size in the form and we'll confirm the minimum for the pieces you want in your quote. Small crews are welcome.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery usually takes 15 to 20 days after you approve your design and pay the deposit, depending on your order. Your free design arrives within 24 hours of your request. Every order ships on its own by express courier to your door.

Who pays import duties and customs fees?

Orders ship from outside the United States. Any import duties, taxes or customs fees that apply are paid by the customer.

Can I get plain fleece hoodies with a logo?

Yes. Fleece and poly-cotton hoodies are available with your logo.

What if something is wrong with my order?

If it arrives defective, damaged or not matching your approved design, we reprint or replace it. See our returns policy for details.

Who owns my design?

You do. We use your logo and artwork only to make your order.
