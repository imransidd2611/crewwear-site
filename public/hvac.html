What an HVAC crew usually orders

HVAC work swings between hot attics in summer and cold mornings in heating season. Most owners cover both with a few pieces per technician: lightweight long sleeves for summer attic work, a hoodie for cold call-outs, a cap for outdoor installs, and a polo for whoever meets customers or works the office.

What to print

  • Your company name or logo on the chest.
  • Your phone number large across the back, so every van and job site advertises you.
  • Heating and cooling themes, if you want a design that shows what you do.
  • Technician names or crew numbers on the sleeve.

When to order

Heating season brings new hires and extra call-outs. Order four to six weeks ahead so the crew is in uniform when demand peaks. Read how long an order takes and how to uniform a crew.

FAQ

Questions from HVAC crews

Can the design show heating and cooling?

Yes. A frost and flame theme is our most popular HVAC look, and we can match it to your logo colours.

Can I put each technician name on the sleeve?

Yes. Names and crew numbers can be added per person.

Where can I get HVAC shirts and HVAC uniforms with my logo?

CrewWear Custom makes HVAC shirts, HVAC work shirts, hoodies and uniforms with your logo, colours and phone number. Send your logo for a free design within 24 hours.

What should HVAC technicians wear in winter?

Most crews layer a moisture-wicking base, a hoodie for cold call-outs and a cap. A full-print hoodie keeps your brand visible even when the crew is bundled up.

How many uniforms does an HVAC tech need?

A common starting point is two to three tops per technician plus one hoodie, so there is always a clean set between washes.

Can I put my licence number on HVAC uniforms?

Yes. Licence numbers, phone numbers and service lines can be printed on the back or sleeve.

How fast do I get my design?

Within 24 hours of your request. Production starts after you approve it.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery usually takes 15 to 20 days after you approve your design and pay the deposit, depending on your order.

Who pays import duties and customs fees?

Orders ship from outside the United States. Any import duties, taxes or customs fees that apply are paid by the customer.
