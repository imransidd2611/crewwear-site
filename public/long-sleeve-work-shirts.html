Lightweight long sleeves cover arms from the sun and carry your design edge to edge. Free design in 24 hours, no setup fees.

Why crews wear long sleeves

  • They cover the arms from sun and scrapes on outdoor jobs.
  • Lightweight polyester feels cooler than heavy cotton in summer.
  • The sleeves give you more space for your logo, crew numbers or a flag patch.

What ours are

Full-print sublimated polyester long sleeves, made to order. The design is part of the fabric and covers the chest, back and sleeves. Fabric weight and details are confirmed on your quote. See sublimated work shirts for contractors and finished pieces.

Good for these trades

Roofing, landscaping, pressure washing, HVAC and plumbing crews all wear long sleeves for daily work.

What to print

  • Your logo on the chest.
  • Phone number and services on the back.
  • Brand colours across the whole shirt, with contrast side panels.
