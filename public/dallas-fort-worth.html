Why DFW crews dress for heat first

North Texas summers are long and hot, and crews work outside through most of them. Lightweight, moisture-wicking polyester long sleeves and caps cover the sun and dry quickly. A hoodie is for cold starts and the occasional winter freeze. That is the mix most DFW owners start with.
