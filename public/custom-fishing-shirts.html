Custom fishing shirts and jerseys with your logo

Full-print sublimated fishing shirts carry your design edge to edge: sponsor logos, team colours, fish graphics and angler names. Free design in 24 hours, no setup fees.

Why sublimation suits fishing shirts

Dye sublimation prints the design into polyester fibres, so a full-colour graphic covers the chest, back and sleeves and does not crack or peel. Lightweight polyester long sleeves cover the arms from sun on the water. See how sublimated shirts work and sublimation vs screen print.

Who orders them

  • Charter captains and fishing guides who want the whole crew and every client photo to show the business name.
  • Tournament teams and clubs that need matching jerseys with sponsor logos.
  • Bait and tackle shops, marinas and boat dealers with staff uniforms.
  • Fishing groups ordering matching shirts for an event.

What you can print

  • Team or business name and logo, large.
  • Sponsor logos on the sleeves and back.
  • Angler names or numbers.
  • Full-colour fish, water or camo graphics in your brand colours.

How to order

Send your logo, the team or crew size and any sponsor logos. You get a free design within 24 hours, with no setup fees and no obligation. Delivery usually takes 15 to 20 days after design approval and deposit, so order early for tournament dates. Read how long an order takes.
