Landscaping and lawn care shirts in Dallas-Fort Worth

North Texas lawns grow for most of the year, so mowing and lawn care crews work long seasons in the sun. A matching uniform makes a small crew look established on every street.

A long mowing season

Common DFW lawns use Bermuda and St. Augustine grass, which grow from spring through fall, so crews are on the road for most of the year. Lightweight, moisture-wicking shirts, a cap and sun-covering long sleeves are the daily kit.

What to print

  • Your company name and logo on the chest.
  • Services and phone number on the back, such as mowing, edging, mulch and irrigation.
  • Your brand greens or colours that match your trailer.

When to order

Most owners plan uniforms in winter for spring hiring. Send your logo in January and have the crew ready before the first mow. See how long an order takes and what to wear for landscaping.

Common questions

Where can I get lawn care shirts in Dallas?

CrewWear Custom makes lawn care and landscaping shirts, tees, long sleeves and hoodies with your logo and phone number and ships them to Dallas-Fort Worth.

What should landscapers wear in Texas summer?

Lightweight, moisture-wicking shirts, often long sleeve for sun coverage, plus a cap, durable pants and sturdy boots.

When should a DFW lawn care business order uniforms?

Late winter, before spring hiring. Allow 15 to 20 days after design approval and deposit.

Can I print my services on the uniform?

Yes. The back is the best place for services and a phone number.
