Roofing shirts in Dallas-Fort Worth

North Texas gets regular spring hail, so roofing companies ramp up fast. Matching shirts help a new crew look established the day it starts.

Hail season hiring

After a big storm, roofers hire quickly and many crews walk neighbourhoods meeting homeowners. A recognizable shirt with your company name tells residents who you are. Order before storm season so new hires start in uniform. See how long an order takes.

Dressing for the sun

Roofs get very hot in a Texas summer. Lightweight, moisture-wicking long sleeves cover the arms from direct sun, and a cap helps. Many crews wear tees on the hottest days and a hoodie for early starts.

What to print

  • A big back print with your company name and phone number, visible from the street.
  • A smaller chest logo.
  • Colour blocking that matches your trucks and yard signs.
  • Foreman and estimator polos with a different colour so customers know who leads the job.

Delivery to DFW

We make every order to your design and ship it to your door in the Dallas-Fort Worth area. Delivery usually takes 15 to 20 days after design approval and deposit. Orders ship from outside the United States, so duties or taxes, if any, are paid by the customer. See how long an order takes.
