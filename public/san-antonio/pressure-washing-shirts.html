Pressure washing shirts in San Antonio

A matching uniform makes your crew easy to spot and easy to trust at the customer's driveway.

Working in San Antonio

Hard water, dust and strong sun leave driveways, patios and siding dirty, so San Antonio washing crews are busy through the warm months.

What to wear

Moisture-wicking long sleeves and tees that dry quickly, plus a cap for sun. A sublimated print is part of the fabric, so it holds up through frequent washing. See sublimation vs screen print.

What to print

  • Logo and company name on the chest.
  • Services on the back, such as driveways, siding, roofs, decks and fences, with your phone number.
  • Crew numbers and contrast panels for a team look.
