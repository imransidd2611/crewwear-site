What to order

Gear guide for roofing crews

What a roofing crew usually orders

Roofing crews work in direct sun and move constantly. Most owners choose lightweight long sleeves for sun coverage, tees for the hottest days, a cap, and a hoodie for early starts and cooler months. Foremen and estimators often get polos.

What to print

  • A big back print with your company name and phone number, visible from the ground and the street.
  • A smaller chest logo.
  • Colour blocking that matches your trucks and yard signs.

Warm states vs cold states

Roofers in Texas, Florida, Arizona and Georgia work through winter, so they order year round. In colder states the busy season is spring to fall, and many owners order in winter to be ready. See winter work gear for trade crews.

FAQ

Questions from roofing crews

Is the fabric breathable?

Yes. Our polyester garments are lightweight and moisture-wicking. Exact fabric details are confirmed on your quote.

Can I get different looks for crew and office?

Yes. Mix garments and designs in one order.

Where can I get roofing shirts and roofing apparel with my logo?

CrewWear Custom makes roofing shirts, hoodies and apparel with your logo, colours and phone number, including lightweight long sleeves for sun. The design is free within 24 hours.

What shirts do roofers wear in hot weather?

Lightweight, moisture-wicking long sleeves cover the arms from the sun while staying breathable. Full-print polyester is a common choice.

Can roofing uniforms have my yard sign colours?

Yes. We build the design around your logo and brand colours so crew, trucks and signs match.

Do you make safety vests for roofing crews?

We offer a neon safety vest with your logo printed on it. If your jobs require certified Class 2 or Class 3 vests, check requirements first. See custom safety vests for contractors.

How fast do I get my design?

Within 24 hours of your request. Production starts after you approve it.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery usually takes 15 to 20 days after you approve your design and pay the deposit, depending on your order.

Who pays import duties and customs fees?

Orders ship from outside the United States. Any import duties, taxes or customs fees that apply are paid by the customer.
