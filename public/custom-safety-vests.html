Custom safety vests with your logo

A bright neon vest with your company logo on the back puts your brand on every job. Free design in 24 hours, no setup fees.

What you get

  • Neon yellow-green mesh vest with silver reflective strips.
  • Your logo large on the back and small on the chest.
  • Your phone number under the logo if you want it.
  • Front zip closure and pockets.

Who orders them

HVAC, plumbing, roofing and landscaping crews that work in yards, driveways and near traffic. A vest goes over any shirt or hoodie, so it brands the whole crew without replacing what they already wear. See also HVAC, roofing and landscaping crews.

Check certification first

Some jobs, clients and states require vests that meet a recognised high-visibility standard (for example ANSI/ISEA 107 Class 2 or 3) with a test report. Our neon vest is for visibility and branding. Tell us your requirements before you order and we will say what we can and cannot supply. Read custom safety vests for contractors.

Send your logo and your crew size. You get a free design within 24 hours, with no setup fees and no obligation. Delivery usually takes 15 to 20 days after design approval and deposit.
