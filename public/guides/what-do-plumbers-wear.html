What do plumbers wear to work?

Plumbers work in tight, wet spaces and then walk into a customer's home. Their clothes have to survive the job and still look professional at the door.

The everyday kit

Most plumbers wear a durable work shirt or polo, sturdy work pants, and boots with good grip. Long sleeves protect the arms in crawl spaces and under sinks. A cap keeps sun and dust off outdoors. Many keep a spare shirt in the truck.

Fabric matters

Wet work favours fabric that dries quickly. Polyester dries faster than heavy cotton, which stays damp. Some plumbers still prefer cotton for softness, so it comes down to the job and personal choice. Read polyester vs cotton work shirts.

Look professional at the door

A matching shirt with your company name and phone number tells customers who you are before you speak. See what to put on the back of a work shirt and our plumbing uniforms.

Common questions

Do plumbers wear uniforms?

Many plumbing companies do. A matching uniform helps customers recognise the technician and builds trust in their home.

What should plumbers not wear?

Loose, dangling clothing that can catch on tools or pipes, and open-toe shoes.

Should plumbers wear long sleeves?

Many do for protection in crawl spaces and under sinks, though some choose short sleeves in heat.
