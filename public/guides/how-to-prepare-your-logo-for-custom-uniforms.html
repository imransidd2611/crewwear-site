How to prepare your logo for custom uniforms

A clean logo file gets you a better design faster. You do not need to be a designer.

The best files to send

  • Vector files such as SVG, AI, EPS or PDF. These scale to any size without blur.
  • High-resolution PNG with a transparent background, if you have no vector file.

If you only have a photo or low-quality image

Send what you have. A photo of a truck wrap, a business card or a screenshot can still work, and we can rebuild a clean version. A clearer image gives a better result.

Tell us your colours

Send your brand colours if you know them. If not, say them in words, for example navy blue and orange, and we will match them from your logo.

Next step

Send your logo through the form. You get a free design within 24 hours with no obligation.

Common questions

What file type is best for a logo?

Vector files such as SVG, AI, EPS or PDF are best. A high-resolution PNG with a transparent background also works.

Can you make a logo from scratch?

Send your name and ideas in the form and ask. We can discuss options.

Do I need to send brand colours?

It helps, but we can match colours from your logo or from a description.
