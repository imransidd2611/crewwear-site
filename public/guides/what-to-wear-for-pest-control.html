What to wear for pest control

Pest control technicians go into homes and crawl spaces. Clothing needs to be practical and the uniform needs to build trust.

Practical kit

Long sleeves and long pants protect against dirt, insects and product contact, plus sturdy closed shoes. Techs use any protective equipment required by the products they apply and local rules; follow product labels and your licence requirements.

Trust at the door

Homeowners want to know who is entering their home. A clean uniform with your company name and tech name helps. See pest control uniforms.

What to print

Company name and logo on the chest, technician name, and phone number on the back.
