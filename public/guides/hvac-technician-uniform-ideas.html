HVAC technician uniform ideas

A good HVAC uniform works in an attic and still looks professional at the front door.

What techs wear

Lightweight long sleeves for summer, a hoodie or layer for winter call-outs, a cap for outdoor work and a polo for estimators.

Design ideas

  • A blue-and-white theme that suggests cooling, or warm accents for heating.
  • A clean chest logo with your phone number big on the back.
  • Technician names for a personal touch.
  • Colours that match your vans.

Common questions

What do HVAC techs wear?

Work shirts or polos, long sleeves in summer, layers in winter, and a cap.

Should HVAC uniforms be dark?

Dark hides dirt but absorbs heat. Mid-tones are a common compromise.

Can I add my licence number?

Yes, on the sleeve or back, if you want it shown.
