Why trade crews wear long sleeves

It looks backwards in summer, but long sleeves are standard on outdoor job sites. Here is why, and what to look for.

The reasons

  • Sun: covered arms burn less over a full day outside.
  • Scrapes: sleeves protect from branches, edges and rough materials.
  • Appearance: a matching long sleeve looks professional and carries more of your design.

What works in hot weather

Lightweight, moisture-wicking polyester feels cooler than heavy cotton and dries fast. See long sleeve work shirts with your logo and sublimated work shirts for contractors.

How often to replace work shirts

Replace them when the fabric thins, the print fades badly or fit no longer suits the person. Many owners reorder before hiring season so new staff are in uniform on day one. Read sizing for a trade crew.
