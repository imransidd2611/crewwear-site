How much do custom work uniforms cost?

The honest answer is that it depends on five things. Know them and you can compare any two quotes fairly, from any supplier.

The five things that set the price

  1. The garment. A tee costs less than a long sleeve, a long sleeve costs less than a hoodie, and a hoodie costs less than a jacket. Fabric weight matters too.
  2. The quantity. Larger orders bring the price per piece down. A crew of 10 is priced differently from a crew of 40.
  3. The print method. Some methods charge per colour or per print location. Full-print sublimation does not, because the whole garment is printed in one pass. See sublimation vs screen print.
  4. The design. A logo on the chest is quicker than a full-body design with a back print, a sleeve print and a phone number.
  5. Shipping and duties. Always ask whether delivery is included, and who pays import duties and taxes.

Hidden costs to ask about

Before you accept any quote, ask these questions. A low price can come with extras that arrive later.

  • Is there a setup, screen or digitising fee for my logo?
  • Is there a per-colour or per-location charge?
  • Is design work free, and how many changes are included?
  • Is shipping included, and are duties and taxes included?
  • What is the minimum order, and what happens if I need one more size?
