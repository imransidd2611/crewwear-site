Uniform rental vs buying your own uniforms

Owners often ask how national uniform services work and what they cost. The honest answer is that it depends on the contract, so here is what to compare.

How a rental program works

A rental service supplies garments, collects them, launders them and replaces worn pieces for a recurring fee. You usually sign a contract for a set term. Ask for the full price list and the exit terms in writing before you agree.

How buying your own works

You pay once for garments made with your logo, colours and phone number, and the crew owns them. You wash them yourself and reorder when you hire or when pieces wear out. See how to uniform a trade crew.

What to compare

  • Total cost over a year, including any fees, not the price of one shirt.
  • Contract length and what happens if your crew size changes.
  • How much of the design is yours: logo placement, colours, back print.
  • Who handles lost, damaged or new-hire garments.

Who buys instead of renting

Small crews in HVAC, plumbing, roofing, landscaping and pressure washing often prefer to own a branded set, because the back print works as advertising on every job. Send your logo for a free design.
