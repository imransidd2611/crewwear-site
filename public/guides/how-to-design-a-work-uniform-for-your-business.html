How to design a work uniform for your business

You do not need a designer. Five decisions get you a uniform that looks professional and advertises your business.

1. Pick the pieces

Decide what each person wears: a shirt, a hoodie, a cap, maybe a vest. Start with the pieces worn every day.

2. Pick colours

Use your logo colours and match your trucks and signs. Two or three colours look cleaner than five.

3. Place the logo

Chest logo on the front, bigger design on the back. Keep it readable from a few metres away.

4. Add the back print

Business name, phone number and services. Read what to put on the back of a work shirt.

5. Prepare your logo file

A clear logo file avoids delays. See how to prepare your logo.
