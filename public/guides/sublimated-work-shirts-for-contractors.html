Sublimated work shirts for contractors

Sublimated shirts carry your design edge to edge, in any number of colours, and the print is part of the fabric. Here is what they are and who they suit.

What a sublimated work shirt is

Dye sublimation prints your design with heat so the ink bonds into polyester fibres. Because it prints the whole garment, the design can run across the chest, back, sleeves and sides in as many colours as you like. It works on polyester, not cotton. See sublimation vs screen print.

Who it suits

  • Roofing crews: lightweight long sleeves with a big back print for the sun.
  • Pressure washing and landscaping: moisture-wicking polyester with services and a phone number across the back.
  • HVAC and plumbing: matching long sleeves and hoodies with a design that shows what you do.

What you can print

  • Company logo and name, large.
  • Phone number and services on the back.
  • Brand colours as a full background, with contrast side panels.
  • Crew numbers or first names on the sleeve.

What to check before ordering

  • Fabric weight and feel. Ask for it on your quote.
  • Sizing. See work uniform sizing.
  • Logo files. See how to prepare your logo.

Common questions

What is a sublimated work shirt?

A shirt where the design is printed with dye sublimation, so the colour bonds into polyester fibres and covers the garment edge to edge.

Can sublimated shirts be printed on cotton?

No. Sublimation works on polyester. Cotton garments use other print methods.

Do sublimated shirts have a setup fee?
