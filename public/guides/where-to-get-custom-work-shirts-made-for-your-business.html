Where to get custom work shirts made for your business

Owners ask this in every trade forum. There are four common routes, and each fits a different situation.

1. Online print-on-demand sites

You design in a browser and order small quantities. It is quick for a first order, but you pay for each print location, and large back designs usually cost more.

2. A local print or embroidery shop

You can see samples and talk to a person. Prices and turnaround vary, and many shops charge setup fees for logos.

3. Uniform rental companies

The company supplies, washes and replaces uniforms on a contract. It suits businesses that want service rather than ownership, but you do not own the design or the garments the same way.

4. A factory-direct supplier

You order made-to-order pieces from a supplier that makes them. This is where full-print sublimated gear comes from. Delivery takes longer, usually 15 to 20 days at CrewWear Custom after design approval and deposit, and import duties and taxes are paid by the customer.

What to ask any supplier

  • Are there setup, screen or digitising fees for my logo?
  • Is design work free, and how many changes are included?
  • What is the minimum order?
  • Is shipping included, and who pays duties and taxes?
  • What happens if an order arrives wrong or damaged?

Common questions

Where can I get custom shirts for my small business?

Common options are online print-on-demand sites, local print or embroidery shops, uniform rental companies and factory-direct suppliers. The best one depends on quantity, turnaround and how much design you need.

Is it cheaper to order from a local shop or online?

It depends on quantity, number of print locations and setup fees. Ask each supplier for the same order and compare the total, including shipping.

Can I get one shirt made, or do I need a bulk order?

Minimums vary by supplier and product. Tell us your crew size in the form and we will confirm the minimum for the pieces you want.
