Winter work gear for trade crews

Cold months are busy for HVAC and plumbing, and many landscapers switch to snow removal. Here is how crews layer.

The three-layer approach

  • Base layer: a moisture-wicking long sleeve that keeps sweat off the skin.
  • Mid layer: a hoodie or fleece for warmth.
  • Outer layer: a jacket or vest for wind and visibility.

Keep your brand visible when bundled up

When crews are covered by outer layers, the hoodie back print and a branded cap are what customers see. Print your name and phone number large on both.

Which trades order in winter

HVAC and plumbing see demand rise in cold weather. In warm states, roofing stays busy all winter. Landscapers who run snow removal need warm layers and a visibility vest. See also HVAC and plumbing uniforms.

Common questions

What should HVAC technicians wear in cold weather?

A moisture-wicking base layer, a hoodie for warmth and a cap. Add an outer layer for wind on exposed jobs.
