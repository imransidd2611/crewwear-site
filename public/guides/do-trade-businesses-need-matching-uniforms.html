Do trade businesses need matching uniforms?

No law requires it, but many successful service businesses do it. Here is why it pays off.

They build trust at the door

Homeowners are letting a stranger into their house. A crew in matching, clean uniforms with the company name looks like an established business, not a one-off visitor.

They make every job a billboard

A name and phone number on the back of each shirt advertises you on every street, every driveway and every parking lot your crew visits.

They help your crew

Matching gear makes a crew feel like a team. It also makes new hires easy to spot on a job site, and it saves workers from wearing their own clothes to messy jobs.

How to start

Start with a shirt or hoodie and a cap for each person. Add polos for the office and an outer layer later. See how to uniform a trade crew, then get a free design.

Common questions

Are uniforms a tax deduction?

Rules vary. Ask your accountant whether branded work clothing is deductible for your business.

Do matching uniforms help get more customers?

They make your business look established and put your phone number in front of people on every job, which helps customers remember and call you.

How many pieces should I start with?

Two to three tops and a cap per person is a common start. Add outer layers and polos later.
