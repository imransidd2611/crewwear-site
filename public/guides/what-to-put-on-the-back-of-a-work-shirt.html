What to put on the back of a work shirt

The back of your crew shirt is the part customers and neighbours see most. Use it like a small billboard.

The five things worth printing

  1. Company name or logo, large and readable from a distance.
  2. Phone number, big enough to read from the curb.
  3. Main services, in a short line such as "Heating, cooling and repair".
  4. Licence or insured line, if customers or your state expect it.
  5. Website or one short tagline, but not both if it makes the print crowded.

What to leave off

  • Long paragraphs. Nobody reads them from a driveway.
  • More than two or three fonts or colours.
  • Small text that disappears at a distance.

Why full-print helps

With full-print sublimation, the design covers the whole back and sleeves, so you can use your brand colours as a background and keep the text bold. See sublimation vs screen print, then send your logo for a free design.

Common questions

How big should a phone number be on a work shirt?

Large enough to read from about 15 feet. Most crews make it one of the biggest elements on the back.

Should I put my website on the back?

Only if there is room. A phone number is usually more useful than a web address for service businesses.

Can names go on the front?

Yes. First names or crew numbers on the chest or sleeve are common.
