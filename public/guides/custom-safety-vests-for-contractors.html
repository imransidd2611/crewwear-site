Custom safety vests for contractors

A branded vest adds visibility and puts your logo on every job. Check your requirements before you order.

When a vest helps

Vests make crews easy to see near traffic, in yards and on busy sites. They also carry a large back logo, so they brand the crew without replacing the shirt underneath.

Certification: check first

Some jobs, clients and states require vests that meet a recognised high-visibility standard (for example ANSI/ISEA 107 Class 2 or 3) with a test report. If yours do, confirm the certification before you order. Our neon safety vest is for visibility and branding, so tell us your requirements and we will say what we can and cannot supply.

Logo placement

The usual layout is a large logo on the back between the reflective strips and a small logo on the left chest. Keep text bold and short.

How to order

Send your logo and tell us your crew size. You get a free design within 24 hours, and no setup fee.

Common questions

Do I need a certified safety vest?

It depends on your job, client and state. Check the requirements for each job before ordering.

Where does the logo go on a safety vest?

Most crews put a large logo on the back and a small one on the left chest.

Can I put my phone number on a vest?

Yes. A phone number below the logo on the back is common.
