What to wear when pressure washing

Pressure washing throws spray and debris. Dress for wet, sun and safety.

Clothing

Moisture-wicking long sleeves and quick-drying pants dry faster than heavy cotton. Waterproof boots with good grip help on wet surfaces.

Safety gear

Eye protection and hearing protection are common, and gloves help with grip. Follow your equipment maker's guidance. Skin can be injured by high-pressure water, so never point the wand at anyone.
