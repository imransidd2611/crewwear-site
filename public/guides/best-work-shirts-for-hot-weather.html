Best work shirts for hot weather

Hot weather work is about staying covered and dry, not just wearing less. The right shirt makes a long day easier.

What to look for

Lightweight fabric that dries quickly, a relaxed fit that lets air move, and a design without heavy seams. Moisture-wicking polyester is a common choice because it carries sweat away from the skin and dries faster than heavy cotton.

Short or long sleeves

Long sleeves cover the arms from direct sun and many outdoor crews prefer them when the fabric is light. Short sleeves can feel cooler in still air. Choose what your crew will actually wear. See long sleeve work shirts.

Colour

Lighter colours reflect more heat than very dark ones, though a light shirt shows dirt faster. Many crews choose mid-tones with dark accents.

Common questions

Are long sleeves hotter?

Not necessarily. A lightweight, breathable long sleeve can feel comfortable and protects from sun.

Is polyester good in heat?

Moisture-wicking polyester dries quickly, which many workers prefer in heat. Comfort is personal.

What colour is coolest?

Lighter colours reflect more sunlight than dark ones.
