How to uniform a trade crew

Most owners order once, run short on sizes, and reorder in a panic. Here is the order that avoids it.

Step 1: Decide what each person wears

A workable starting point per crew member is a mix of base layers for the season and one outer layer.

  • Two to three long sleeves or tees, so there is always a clean one.
  • One hoodie for cold mornings.
  • One cap if the crew works outdoors.
  • A polo for whoever meets customers at the door or in the office.

Step 2: Put the right things on it

  • Company name or logo on the chest.
  • Phone number large on the back. Your crew is a moving ad on every street they work.
  • Licence number if your state or customers expect it.
  • One colour system across everything, so mixed pieces still look like one team.

See how this looks on finished pieces in the gallery.

Step 3: Get sizes right the first time

Collect sizes from every person before you place the order. Add a few spare pieces in common sizes for new hires. Ask for a size chart before ordering and confirm whether sizes run closer to US or Asian cuts.

Step 4: Plan the reorder

New hires, wear and weather all mean you will reorder. Keep your approved design on file so a reorder uses the exact same artwork and colours. With us, the approved design is stored so you can order again without starting over.

Common questions

How many shirts should each worker get?

A common starting point is two to three tops per person plus one outer layer, so there is always a clean set between washes.

What should go on the back of a work shirt?

Most trade owners put a large company name and phone number on the back, since that is what customers and neighbours see most.

Can I reorder the same design later?

Yes. Your approved design is kept so new hires can be matched to the same artwork and colours.
