Plumber shirts and uniforms in Houston

Homeowners let plumbers into kitchens and bathrooms. A clean matching uniform builds trust before the technician says a word.

Why the uniform matters for Houston plumbers

Houston plumbers handle everything from water heaters and slab leaks to flood clean-up after heavy rain. Many calls are urgent and in people's homes, so a visible company name and phone number on the shirt reassures the customer and helps them remember who to call next time.

What to print

  • Your logo on the chest, kept clean and readable.
  • A large back print with your company name, phone number and "24/7 emergency service" if you offer it.
  • Your licence number if you want it shown. Check Texas plumbing board rules for advertising.
  • Colours that match your vans.

What plumbers wear

Moisture-wicking long sleeves for daily jobs, a hoodie for cool or emergency call-outs, a cap, and polos for estimators and office staff.
