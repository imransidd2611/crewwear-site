Pressure washing shirts in Houston

Humidity feeds mildew and algae on driveways, siding and roofs across Houston, which keeps washing crews busy. A matching uniform makes your crew easy to spot and trust.

Wet, sunny work

Pressure washing is spray, sun and repetition. Moisture-wicking long sleeves dry quickly and cover the arms. A print that is part of the fabric holds up better through frequent washing than a surface print. See sublimation vs screen print.

What to print

  • Your logo and company name on the chest.
  • Services on the back, such as driveways, siding, roofs, decks and fences, with your phone number.
  • Crew numbers and contrast panels for a team look.

Neighbourhood jobs

Much residential work comes from the street. When your crew washes one driveway in a subdivision, neighbours see the shirts. A big back print with your name and number turns each job into advertising.

Delivery to Houston

We make every order to your design and ship it to your door in the Houston area. Delivery usually takes 15 to 20 days after design approval and deposit. Orders ship from outside the United States, so duties or taxes, if any, are paid by the customer. See how long an order takes.
